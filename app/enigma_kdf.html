Enigma KDF

Enigma M4, with the key management modernized. Instead of daily key sheets and transmitted indicators, both sides derive identical rotor and plugboard settings from one shared seed, fresh for every date and message.

Never sent anywhere. Used only to compute today's settings, locally, in this page.
Long memorable phrases are fine here — more unpredictable words beats a short clever one. If you're storing this in a password manager rather than memorizing it, a shorter random string works just as well.
